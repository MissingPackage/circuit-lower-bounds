## 1. Anatomy of Li-Yang

**Potential** (LY Def 3.6, p.14): mu = g + aI*i + aQ*q + aP*Phi.
- g gates, i influential inputs, q quadratic equations.
- Phi = #troubled - |P|, where P is a packing of disjoint adjacent troubled pairs (Defs 3.1-3.5). A troubled gate is an AND-type 1-gate fed by two 2-variables.
- Constants: aP=0.2, aI=9.6, aQ=1.8, delta=12.8.

**LP** (p.19): maximise delta - aP/2 - aI subject to delta <= aI + min{aI/3, 2-2aP+aQ, 4-4aP, 3+aP, 5-aQ, (5-2aP+aQ)/2} and aP <= 1/2. The -aP/2 comes from Lemma 3.7: at most n/2 + 5d/2 troubled gates. My grid re-solve gives 3.1 for LY and 3+1/86 for FGHK (rev2 Thm 3.1, p.15).

**Bottleneck structure** (my arithmetic on the published LP; not in the paper). At the optimum four terms tie at 3.2: 4-4aP (4 gates, up to 4 new troubled gates: Cases 3 and 7), 3+aP (Case 5), and the quadratic pair 5-aQ and (5-2aP+aQ)/2.
- Fixing only the 4-gate family, even to zero new troubled gates, leaves exactly 3.1.
- Fixing only the quadratic family (6 gates) also leaves exactly 3.1.
- Fixing both gives 3.1665.
- Ceiling: delta - aI <= 3+aP, and aP <= 1/2 (so Rule 1 cannot raise mu). Together they cap this template at 3 + aP/2 <= **3.25**.

**Normalisation** (§3.3, p.15-16): five rules, each with dPhi <= 1 (Rule 1: <= 2):
1. remove a 0-gate;
2. remove a gate trivialised by a constant input;
3. bypass a degenerate gate fed by a constant;
4. remove a "useless" 1-gate;
5. fix a gate whose two inputs coincide.

Case 0 (p.20) adds global semantic steps. Lemma 3.11 (p.17) bounds each rule (dmu >= 1-2aP or 1-aP) and confines new troubled gates to the inputs of the eliminated gate and their successors.

**Confluence.** Neither paper claims it; FGHK rev2 §3.5 (p.23) "rel[ies] on the specified order of applications". CDJ1 proves convergence for DeMorgan and {AND, XOR, NOT} (Thm 13; its abstract says {AND, OR, XOR}). CDJ1 Observation 16 (§4, p.13): no confluent system exists for U2 or B2, because negations get pushed up or down. CDJ's rules (Defs 2, 6) work on acyclic term graphs and have no Rule 4, no cyclic XOR part and no xor-reconstruction. So: certificates replay the per-rule accounting along one fixed rule order. Whether the eliminated-gate count is independent of order is unknown [VERIFY].

## 2. Certificate for one case

A certificate is (P, pre, sigma, claim):
- **P**: a finite labelled fragment. Variables carry a degree class {0, 1, 2, 3, 4+}, protected or free, and couple edges. Gates carry AND/XOR and a degree class. P also has open ports and identity/non-identity constraints.
- **pre**: the earlier-case negations that can be expressed on P.
- **sigma**: at most 3 substitutions.
- **claim**: dmu per substitution is at least a linear form in (aI, aQ, aP).

"All completions" means every fair, normalised semicircuit with an rdq-source and empty packing that embeds P and satisfies pre. That set is infinite. dmu depends only on:
- gates removed inside P (extra cascades each give >= 1-2aP >= 0, so ignoring them is sound);
- dPhi, which by Lemma 3.11 changes only within radius +1 of the eliminated set;
- the variables and couples inside P.

So the check reduces to enumerating radius-(r+1) completions under degree-class abstraction, up to isomorphism. This is finite if degree classes are exact wherever edges are lost [VERIFY per case].

Case 8 is not finite because of the paths. Fix: treat xor-reconstruction as a trusted macro given by FGHK rev2 Lemma 3.4.2 (p.20): new Z with in-degree 2, out(G)+1, out(x1)=0, out(Z)=out(x1)-1, all other degrees unchanged. Fairness (a global rank condition) becomes that lemma's axiom.

From the invariant the checker needs only "the output is never trivialised". Case 0.3 is not locally checkable; dropping it is sound and the leaves that fail only because of it are reported.

## 3. Search problem

The problem is max over a, min over patterns, max over sigma, min over completions, of a linear form in a.
- With sigma fixed per pattern: an LP.
- Joint sigma: a MILP, i.e. measure-and-conquer weight tuning.

The case tree comes from failure-driven refinement: start at a minimal AND-gate and its inputs; try each sigma; on failure, split an attribute or expand a node; stop at a radius cap. Uncertified leaves at the cap are the bottlenecks.

**Size** [estimate]:
- Labels up to free negation: a gate is AND/XOR × 4 degree classes = 8; a variable is 10.
- **Radius 1:** about 105 input pairs × 4 degree classes for G × up to 4 successors × up to 2^8 cross edges ≈ 10^7 labelled patterns, about 10^5 up to isomorphism before case pruning.
- **Radius 2:** about 20 more nodes with 18 labels each, plus identifications, ≈ 10^25 × Bell factors. Only lazy expansion is feasible.

**Blow-ups:**
1. node identifications (Case 5.1 B=C);
2. unbounded fan-out;
3. XOR paths and fairness;
4. cascades and rule order;
5. multi-step sigma;
6. couples linking distant variables.

## 5. Recommendation

Compute is small; the checker is the cost.

**(a) DEFAULT: FGHK first.**
- **WP3.1:** checker plus FGHK rev2 Cases 1-5 (15 leaves, no xor-reconstruction).
  - Predictions: all verify; at least 10 mutants are rejected; the LP over the certified gains gives 3+1/86.
- **WP3.2:** Cases 6-7 via the Lemma 3.4.2 macro.
  - Prediction: the 2015 Case 7.2.4.2 is flagged and rev2 passes. This is the only known ground-truth error.
- **Budget:** <= 50 CPU-h on 2 cores, <= 1 CPU-h per leaf.
- **Kill if:** more than 20% of leaves need per-case code, any mutant is accepted, or the gap is not flagged.

**(b) LY directly.**
- **WP:** LY Case 5 (19 leaves) at (0.2, 9.6, 1.8). Prediction: all verify.
- **Budget:** about 2x (a). Kill: as (a).
- **Risks:** no known error to calibrate against; 8.2.5.3/8.2.5.4 need new proofs.

**(c) Potential search without a fixed case analysis.**
- **WP:** refinement on the fragment where the minimal AND-gate is fed by variables; LY template; radius cap 3.
  - Prediction: at aP=0 the only uncertified radius-1 structure is the troubled gate, and 3+aP is reproduced.
- **Budget:** <= 200 CPU-h (1 day on a 12-core Mac).
- **Kill if:** the troubled gate is not rediscovered, or more than 10^6 leaves are open at the cap.
- **Ceiling:** this template cannot pass 3.25. Fixing one bottleneck family yields exactly 3.1. So (c) pays only once it searches over new measure terms.

**Default: (a), then (b) on the same checker; (c) only after WP3.2 passes.**
